## A deferred decision may not reopen what a criterion already fixed — scan the Deferred list against the criteria before returning

A Deferred Decisions bullet is read as licence to choose either way at plan time, so if a
criterion has already pinned one side (the budget is stated in words; a block ends at the next
heading), the bullet contradicts it and the looser reading wins. Before returning, pair every
deferred bullet with the criteria that touch the same value and narrow it to what is genuinely
open — the computation or the name, never the observable unit or boundary the criteria state.
