## Write Scope/Out as bare boundary bullets; justify at most the one boundary that is genuinely surprising

The section contract says state the boundary, not its defence, and a defence sentence per bullet is where a PRD silently goes over budget — six bullets become eighteen lines saying what is already implied by the exclusion. Name the excluded surface and stop. Reserve a single trailing clause for the one exclusion a reader would otherwise read as an oversight, and let Assumptions & Risks carry anything that needs a real argument.
