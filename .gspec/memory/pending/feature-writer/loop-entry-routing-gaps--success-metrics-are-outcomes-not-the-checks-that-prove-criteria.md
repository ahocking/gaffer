## Write Success Metrics as feature-level outcomes, and never defer what Scope already decided

A success metric is the observable result the feature exists to produce (the artifact parses, the sweep's green result is evidence again), not the acceptance check that proves a criterion — if a metric can be pasted under a capability as a bullet, it is a restatement. Likewise, a bullet under Deferred Decisions must be genuinely open: a boundary already stated as Out belongs there once, with its reason appended to that Out bullet, not repeated as a deferral. Before returning, read each metric and each deferred bullet against the Capabilities and Scope sections and delete any that duplicates them.
