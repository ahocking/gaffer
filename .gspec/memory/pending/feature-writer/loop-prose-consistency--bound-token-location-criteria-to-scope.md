## Bound any acceptance criterion that counts occurrences to the surfaces Scope admits

A criterion of the form "X appears in exactly N places and nowhere else" is a claim about the
whole repository unless the criterion itself names the files it scans — and stray occurrences in
comments, sibling scripts, or completed records that Scope excludes make the case fail on day one
or force edits an immutability floor refuses. Write the scan bound into the criterion ("across
<the named surfaces>, in exactly the three …"), not into the reader's assumed context.
