## Check every universal quantifier in Success Metrics and Capabilities against the exceptions the capabilities themselves preserve

A metric or criterion phrased "no X is ever Y" is the easiest sentence to over-reach on: the capabilities usually keep a residue (unknown-forever cases, blocked expiry, an unfinished dependency) that the metric then contradicts. Narrow the quantifier to the set the feature actually closes and name the remaining class as a Scope/Deferred bullet, rather than letting a headline promise more than the acceptance criteria deliver.
